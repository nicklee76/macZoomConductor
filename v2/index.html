Core Capabilities

A Smarter, Faster Way to Browse

Everything you need to manage your windows, tabs, and displays without friction or clutter.

Custom Zoom Rules & Display Baselines

Set your preferred default zoom level for each screen. Create custom Zoom Rules for specific websites, displays, or window names (e.g. 125% for reading on 4K, 100% lock for maps/design tools) so your pages always display at your ideal size.

Custom Zoom Rules

Find Any Tab by What’s Inside the Page

Can’t remember the exact title or URL? Simply type a word or phrase you remember reading on the page. browserNexus locates the exact tab and highlights the matching sentence.

Smart Page Search

Visual Page Previews at a Glance

Hover over any tab in your list to see a quick visual snapshot of the rendered page before switching to it—saving you from clicking back and forth through dozens of open windows.

Hover Snapshots

One-Click Background Audio Muting

Easily spot which tab is playing sound in the background and mute or unmute it with a single click without having to hunt through your open windows across Safari, Chrome, and Firefox.

Quick Audio Control

Name & Organize Your Workspaces

Label your browser windows with friendly names like "Work Projects", "Research", or "Travel". Your custom names stay saved even after you quit and reopen your browser.

Window Organization

Effortless Tab Drag & Drop

Reorder tabs within a window or drag them smoothly between different windows across your browsers to keep your multi-tasking workspace clean and structured.

Tab Management

One Shortcut for All Your Browsers

Press a single global keyboard shortcut (⇧⌘A) from anywhere on your Mac to summon your complete browser overview, jump to any tab instantly, or navigate smoothly with your keyboard.

Quick Navigation (⇧⌘A)

Browse Session History & Restore Workspaces

Accidentally closed a research window or lost tabs during a restart? browserNexus keeps a timeline of your recent browsing sessions, letting you inspect past tab layouts and restore entire workspaces with one click.

Session Recovery

Your Privacy Comes First

We respect your privacy. All your browsing activity, open tabs, and window layouts stay strictly on your Mac. Optional features like page searching only activate with your explicit approval.

Privacy by Design
